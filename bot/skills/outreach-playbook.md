# Outreach playbook

Use after `research_lead` returns `found: true`.

## Structure

1. **Hook** — one concrete signal (funding, hiring, product launch, expansion).
2. **Bridge** — why that signal creates a relevant problem your product fits.
3. **Proof** — one short, honest product-fit sentence from research (no fake logos).
4. **CTA** — exactly one ask, and only in the `cta` field when saving.
   Prefer a yes/no or calendar-light ask. Body copy ends on the bridge/proof —
   never on a question.

## Do

- Mirror the lead's world (title, company stage, geography).
- Keep email body ~90–140 words; LinkedIn ~60–110 words.
- Cite the signal in language close to the research summary.

## Do not

- Invent metrics, customers, compliance claims, or mutual connections.
- Apologize for interrupting or use "I hope this email finds you well".
- Put `?` in the body, stack CTAs, offer an alternate ask, or attach a deck
  unprompted.
